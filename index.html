About me

I did my secondary education in Luxembourg, where I learned Java and first worked with computers as something to program and build rather than only use. Portuguese is my native language, and I also speak Luxembourgish, English, French and German.

I like to understand how a system works before I try to secure it. In practice that means setting it up, changing it, breaking it on purpose and then reading the logs to see what happened. At home I run a small lab of three mini PCs and a managed switch for exactly that. At school I work through labs on Linux, Windows Server and Active Directory, and networking.

I am a student, and I am building these skills step by step. I document what I do and keep it on GitHub so that it can be checked.

Linux and system administration
